# Lore & seasons — Chud

> *"You think this is just a face-scanning game, Morty? It's a*
> *MYTHOLOGY, Morty. Every season is a new saga. Every cosmetic is a*
> *relic. The man-animals will fight over the Frieza arc cosmetics like*
> *baby Psychlos fight over the last kerbango bottle. That's the game,*
> *that's the *bzrp* whole game."* — Terl, head of narrative

The game has a story even if 90% of players never read it. The story is what gives meaning to limited-time cosmetics, faction wars, and seasonal events. Without lore, FOMO is just FOMO. With lore, FOMO is *participation*.

## Universe primer

The world of Chud is split between two energies:

- **Aura** — the radiant force of being unbothered, hyped, certified Saint. Channeled by Glazers.
- **Bricks** — the heavy, swampy force of cringe given form. Channeled by Chuds.

Both forces are real. Both forces are necessary. The eternal calibration between them is what keeps the timeline stable. (Yes, we're stealing from every shonen anime simultaneously. That's the point.)

## Season 1: "The Saiyan Saga" (weeks 1-8)

> *In the beginning, there was only Aura. Then someone said something cringe. And the Bricks were born.*

- **Onboarding season.** All new mechanics introduced gradually week-by-week.
- **Form unlocks**: Power Level → Super Saiyan at 10k aura, SSJ2 at 50k, SSJ3 at 200k
- **Mid-season event**: "The First Brick" — global counter showing collective L's deployed worldwide. When it hits the threshold, the chud-side meta unlocks and Goblin Hour goes live.
- **End-season tournament**: 256-bracket "World Tournament," winner gets a season-exclusive mythic aura `aura_world_tournament_winner_s1`.
- **Battle pass theme**: martial arts gi colors, scouter HUDs, gold rings

## Season 2: "The Frieza Arc" (weeks 9-16)

> *They came from across the galaxy. They were extremely cringe. They had numbers.*

- **Villain season.** Chud-side cosmetics dominate the pool.
- **New mechanic**: "Form" reveals — chud-side transformations are now playable (chud-form 1, 2, 3, Final Form)
- **Mid-season event**: "Frieza's Invasion" — random global push notif: "the planet has 5 minutes." Massive 5-minute event where every player who scans gets bonus pull tickets.
- **Lore drop**: secret "Goldspot" cosmetic only unlockable by glazing during an active Frieza Invasion event.
- **End-season**: faction war — Glazers vs Chuds, full week, winning side gets a permanent profile flair.

## Season 3: "Cell Games" (weeks 17-24)

> *Tournament season. Everyone's invited. Most of you will lose.*

- **Tournament-heavy.** Daily mini-brackets, weekly squad brackets, season-long ladder.
- **New mechanic**: "Cell-style absorption" — beat a player in battle, *temporarily* equip one of their cosmetics for 24h
- **End-season**: The Cell Games — week-long elimination tournament for top 1024 players globally

## Season 4: "Buu Saga" (weeks 25-32)

> *Chaos season. The rules slacken. Strange things drop from the sky.*

- **All-out chaos.** Rate limits softened. Goblin Hour expanded to 6 hours. Cringe cosmetics doubled in drop rate.
- **New mechanic**: "Fusion" — two squadmates can temporarily merge profiles into a single combined power-level for one battle
- **Mid-season**: random "candy storm" events where chuds get a 5x multiplier for 30 minutes
- **End-season**: "The Reset" — alignment percentages soft-reset, story-justified

## Season 5+: Direction TBD

Roadmap: introduce a *third* faction in season 5 ("The Outsiders" — neither glazer nor chud, plays a different verb). Keep the universe expanding so lore reveals become a retention driver of their own.

## Cross-season constants

- Every season has:
  - A 60-tier battle pass (free + premium tracks)
  - 12 season-exclusive cosmetics that retire forever on day 57
  - One global mid-season event
  - One tournament finale
  - A 1-week "intermission" between seasons for soft-resets and previews

## Why this matters mechanically

Players don't need to *understand* the lore to feel the pressure of "Frieza's Invasion is happening right now, push notif, 5 minutes." They just feel: something important is happening, I'm missing out if I don't open the app, the people on the leaderboard tomorrow will be the people who showed up tonight.

That's the engine. The lore is the wrapper that makes the engine feel like a *story* instead of a treadmill.

## Cosmetic naming convention
Season-exclusives are slugged `[item]_s[N]_[event]` for easy retirement tracking. Example: `aura_world_tournament_winner_s1`, `hat_frieza_third_form_s2`, `overlay_cell_games_finalist_s3`.
