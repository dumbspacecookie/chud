## The name
**Chud.** Four letters, one syllable, unmistakable. Originated as 4chan slur for reactionary online types — partly reclaimed as gen-Z shorthand for "lame/cooked guy." We lean *all the way* into the menace branding because the dual-mode mechanic balances it: every chud-deploy has a glaze counter-deploy. Owning the cringe is the joke.

## The two roles

| | **Glazer** 🔥 | **Chud** 💀 |
|---|---|---|
| Verb | glaze, rizz, hype | chud, brick, ick |
| Currency | **Aura** | **Ls** |
| Energy | hype tunnel | menace tunnel |
| Vibe colors | hot pink → gold → white | piss yellow → swamp green → static |
| Sound palette | choir hits, ki-blast charges, transformation whoosh | dial-up modem, fart horn, womp womp, vine boom |
| Endgame form | Ultra Instinct (gold particles, halo) | Kid Buu (feral pink, glitch artifacts) |
| Avatar trim | gold rings | crusty rings |
| Notification voice | hype best friend | menace gremlin |

## Tone of voice
- **Terse.** Sentences should fit in a notification.
- **Lowercase by default.** Capital letters mean drama.
- **Gen-Z native, never gen-Z impersonating.** No "fellow kids" energy. If you're not sure, cut the slang.
- **Lean into the irony.** The cringe is the joke. The chud knows he is the chud.
- **Never moralize.** Never lecture about "good sportsmanship." The platform is the chaos.

## Visual direction
- **Web 1.0 cringe-pilled aesthetic, deployed knowingly.** Comic Sans appears, but only on chud-side cosmetics. Glazer side is clean, gold-flecked, fight-game UI.
- **The split**: when both modes are visible (e.g. leaderboard), they're rendered in *aggressively* different visual languages so the user understands they're picking a side, not a setting.
- **The glow.** The AR aura is the hero of every screenshot. Particle effects matter more than UI chrome.

## Logo direction
- Wordmark: `chud` lowercase, slightly distressed, with the C as a partial halo and the D as a pixelated brick.
- App icon: a glowing circle (the aura) with a single pixelated brick lodged in it. Reads at 48px.

## Don'ts
- ❌ Don't show the Chud archetype as a real demographic. The chud is a *role you play*, not a *type of person*. All cringe cosmetics target the *role*, not identity groups.
- ❌ Don't ever moralize about who is "really" cringe.
- ❌ Don't capitalize "Chud" except at sentence start. Lowercase always. Brand law.
- ❌ Don't use emoji decoratively in product copy. Reserve 🔥 for aura ranks and 💀 for chud ranks. Two emoji, two meanings.

- **Pokemon GO**: geo-bonus zones (Rizz Spots)

- **Genshin Impact**: gacha pity timer, capsule reveal pacing
- **Yik Yak**: location-based feed for the live discovery surface
- **DBZ**: the entire visual language and progression metaphor (we don't say "DBZ" anywhere, but the players see it instantly)
